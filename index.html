Course Description

Review of vector representation of forces, resultant force and moment, equilibrium of concurrent and nonconcurrent forces, area moments and products of inertia, support reactions, free-body diagrams, internal forces in beams, stress and strain components in solids, Hooke’s law for isotropic solids, bending and shear stresses in beams, beam deflection, pressure vessels, and torsion.

Policies

Collaboration, Exams, and Lateness

Collaboration with other students on homeworks is acceptable; however, each student must solve the problems on their own. Every student must understand their homework submission and be able to explain it to the instructor. Homework must be uploaded electronically to Bruin Learn in PDF format, with file size less than 20 MB.

Late homework is accepted with a penalty of 0.2% for every minute late. For example, a submission due at 11:59 pm and submitted at 12:12 am loses 2.4%. No extension will be granted except in case of family or medical emergency with documentary evidence.

Midterm and final exams are closed-book. Students may bring one letter-sized, two-sided cheat sheet to each exam. You may write or type anything you wish on the cheat sheet. A calculator may be needed for the exams.

In case of acceptable emergencies, any reasonable accommodation as requested by the appropriate UCLA office will be made.
